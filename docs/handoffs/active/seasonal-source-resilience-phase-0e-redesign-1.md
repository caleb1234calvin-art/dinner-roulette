## Why Phase 0E exists

The seasonal discovery work did not fail because the current product hangs. The 20-second server provider-chain budget and 25-second client watchdog are already verified and must remain intact.

Phase 0D showed that a $0 national candidate-discovery layer is plausible, but it did NOT establish a production-worthy composition.

Locked Phase 0D findings:

- Overture processed roughly 4.69 million sampled/regional rows and retained 1,518 topical leads.
- 45 / 129 sampled observations were clear metadata false positives and 40 were ambiguous.
- Of 25 positive diagnostic attractions, 16 matched frozen candidates, 1 was probable, and 8 were misses.
- Only 14 of those diagnostics had matching H/C/P category signals.
- Overture was the strongest measured initial national candidate substrate but did not establish current-season truth.
- OSM remains valuable as an independent baseline/lane, but live Overpass transport was unreliable and mirrors are NOT inventory diversity.
- Reusable government/open datasets may enrich a verification queue, but Phase 0D did not prove a scalable seasonal venue feed.
- Common Crawl/open-web/schema/feed/API families were feasibility findings, not equivalent live-acquisition proof.
- operator/DMO facts were useful for research/verification, but no scalable reusable production-rights path was demonstrated.
- full-U.S. catalog size/churn/refresh/hosting fit remained unmeasured.
- OSM/Overture publication/ODbL architecture remained unresolved.
- explicit negative current-season evidence must outrank generic positive place status and freshness expiry.
- source failure must never become lifecycle negative.
- direct provider spend for the core path remains targeted at $0.

The strongest Phase 0D architecture family was a hybrid:

**reusable national candidate substrate + reproducible geographic partitions + separate rights-qualified claim/verification queue + local-first query + bounded asynchronous enrichment + last-good-catalog behavior.**

Phase 0E exists to close the specific evidence gaps that prevented that design from moving to prototype.

## Mission

Answer one bounded question:

**Can Pick For Us now define a defensible $0 seasonal-discovery prototype that materially improves arbitrary-U.S. H/C/P discovery while keeping uncertainty honest, identity manageable, rights/provenance explicit, and the 20s/25s settlement guarantees intact?**

Phase 0E must close five proof gaps:

### P1 — Candidate classification quality
Can a broad open-place screen produce a useful high-confidence H/C/P candidate tier without the Phase 0D false-positive burden becoming operationally unmanageable?

### P2 — Independent OSM increment
Does a successful bounded OFFLINE OSM extract materially add valid candidates beyond Overture, and what identity/collision burden does the union create?

Do not use live Overpass as the primary evidence mechanism for this proof.

### P3 — Seasonal-truth path
Can at least one rights-qualified automatically discoverable source family, OR a composable registry of such sources, supply reusable current-season claims at useful scale?

### P4 — National packaging / refresh feasibility
Can the candidate index be packaged, partitioned, refreshed and queried at $0 direct provider spend in a way compatible with current/future Pick For Us infrastructure?

### P5 — Licensing architecture closure
Can we choose an engineering path for OSM/Overture that has explicit, bounded obligations rather than an unresolved mixing assumption?

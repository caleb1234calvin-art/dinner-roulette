# Recovery comparator interpretation, pre-live addendum

This clarification applies to frozen protocol a02fac37a8ed0d53d29b5f28e215bb1f984f57fb8a242eb7eae02f69550920ef. It changes no source, case, provider, request/failure budget, deadline or stop rule. Independent review must accept it and the final executable manifest must bind its exact bytes before live entry.

## Numerator and denominator

D's first usable recovered control is the earliest correctly rendered actual control that includes at least one eligible identity affirmed by a successful, physically dispatched live audit after natural primary failure. A pre-existing curated-only Pick, enabled button, raw RPC settlement or eligible-pool observation is not that recovered control. It may be actual Options after an earlier curated Pick. Retain the actual control type, identities/provenance, render time and driver timing diagnostics.

The30-second criterion measures that control's render time minus natural primary-failure settlement time. The separate2× comparison uses D navigation-to-that-render time divided by matched R navigation-to-first-correct-Pick time. Match location, radius and category settings. Kansas City20 has one R observation; Joplin50 uses the median of its two R first-Pick observations. Both values include ordinary app interaction/animation. This is a conservative recovery-usability bound, not a like-for-like comparison of Pick versus Options rendering speed. Do not use it to claim later-control navigation-time superiority.

Unpaired Columbia sentinels and the Joplin20 follow-up have no matched R denominator. Record the ratio as unobserved/not applicable; do not substitute historical or different-radius/location/category values. The30-second requirement still applies. Any missing mandatory main R control remains a main-evidence gap and is not repaired by this exception.

## Failure versus missing evidence

An uncensored observed recovered control exceeding30 seconds from primary failure, or exceeding2× its available matched R denominator, is a failed performance criterion. It is not merely a missing naturalRecovery observation and cannot qualify the run for additional reserve cases to obtain a favorable result. Report every failed-primary acquisition and every recovery outcome, including failures/censors; never retain only the favorable instance.

No natural primary failure means recovery remains unobserved. A global-stop-censored/no-dispatch audit is also unobserved recovery, not success or infinite latency. Existing singleton-gap reserve conditions and irreversible global stop rules remain binding. A fixture/injected failure never becomes naturally failed live recovery.

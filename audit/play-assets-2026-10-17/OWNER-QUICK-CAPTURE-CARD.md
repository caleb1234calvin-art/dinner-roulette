# Pick For Us: the six screenshots to send

Use the authorized **Pick For Us Android debug app**, not the browser or an old install. Keep an internet connection. Pick a public city using the map-pin location control → enter city, region and country → **Set location**. Avoid home/GPS details, notifications and personal messages. Use the same theme and send original screenshot files without cropping or compression.

1. **Dinner preferences.** Tap the top Dinner mode. Show the clearest view of the location, distance, budget and cuisine controls. Don't force the whole scrolling page into one image.
2. **Dinner options.** Tap **Give us options**. Capture the real **Tonight's options** cards. Four is ideal if genuinely available; fewer is fine.
3. **Date Night options.** Close options and tap the top **Date Night** mode. Turn **Spooky Season** off if it is on, choose year-round activities, then **Give us options**. Capture this actual full-screen shortlist. The October panel stays on the home page even when off; don't erase it or change the date.
4. **Nightlife preferences.** Close options, choose **Nightlife**, and show the clearest view of venue types, distance, budget and the Chill–Lively preference.
5. **One Date Night result.** Return to Date Night, open options and tap a real year-round destination. Show its details and directions where they fit. Keep uncertainty notices. A website button may be absent; don't add one.
6. **Favorites.** Tap **Save** on a genuine result, then close it. If an options screen remains, close that too. Tap bottom **Favorites** and capture the saved list. Don't clear private entries or manufacture history to stage this.

Wait for searches and animations to finish; dismiss the keyboard and Quick tour. Use ordinary screenshots, not stitched scrolling captures. If something fails or personal information can't be avoided, pause that shot rather than editing the app content.

Send the originals with your phone model, Android version, chosen city and approximate capture time. No serial number or account details are needed. We can prepare the final dimensions without distorting the UI.

These are phone listing candidates. Tablet testing remains NOT RUN, and final image selection/rights review and release approval remain separate.

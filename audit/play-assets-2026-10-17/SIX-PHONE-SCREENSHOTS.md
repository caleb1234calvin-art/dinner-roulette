# Pick For Us: six Android screenshots

Prepared October 17, 2026. A capture guide for the existing PR78 development app, not evidence that these six screenshots have been taken. Tablet capture/testing remains **NOT RUN**.

## Quick start on your phone

1. Open the already authorized **Pick For Us** debug app, package `app.pickforus.android`. Keep your existing installs and saved data. If you cannot distinguish the intended app, stop and confirm it rather than uninstalling anything.
2. Use a stable internet connection. Pick one non-sensitive public city for this set: tap the map-pin icon (accessibility label **Change location**, tooltip **Enter a location**), replace the prefilled text with the city, region and country, then **Set location**. Confirm the displayed city. Avoid a home address or precise personal GPS position.
3. Use one readable theme consistently. Dismiss the keyboard and notification pop-ups. For **Quick tour**, use its X (**Close hints**) or finish **Next → Got it** before each shot. You can use your normal Do Not Disturb setting yourself if you wish; do not disable connectivity or security protections for the capture.
4. Take ordinary screenshots with your phone's screenshot action, often Power + Volume Down. Send the **original image files**, without messaging-app compression, cropping or markup. Keep them even if later exports need resizing.
5. Follow the six shots below. Scroll normally when needed. Do not zoom out until text becomes unreadable, change the date, edit app content, or combine several scroll positions into a fake screen.

## The six shots, in listing order

### 1. Dinner: make the choice your own

- Tap **Pick** in the bottom navigation if needed, then the top **Dinner** mode button. Use the top mode buttons throughout this guide; the Dinner preset also named Date Night only changes restaurant filters.
- Check the public city under **Searching near**. Set a practical distance under **How far?**, a broad **Budget**, and a genuine cuisine preference under **What sounds good?**. Keep **Favorites only** off for discovery; choose **Open now only** honestly rather than implying all results have verified current hours.
- Capture a natural screen showing the Dinner identity and the clearest preference controls. It is fine if the page needs scrolling; do not squeeze every control into one picture.
- Suggested filename: `01-dinner-preferences-raw.png`.
- Draft alt text: “Dinner preferences with a search location, distance, budget and cuisine choices.” Revise it to match what is actually visible.

### 2. Dinner: up to four real options

- On the same Dinner screen, wait for loading to finish, then tap **Give us options**.
- Capture **Tonight's options** with the genuine restaurant cards. Prefer a useful set of four if the live app supplies it; fewer is valid. Do not duplicate cards to reach four.
- Keep visible hours, distance, uncertainty and other real labels. If all cards do not fit, use a normal viewport; do not stitch separate positions together or crop important notices.
- Filename: `02-dinner-options-raw.png`.
- Draft alt text: “Dinner shortlist with nearby restaurant options and the details supplied by the app.”

### 3. Date Night: evergreen ideas

- Close the options using the X, labelled **Close options**, then tap **Date Night**.
- If **Spooky Season** is on, turn it off using the real switch. Choose ordinary, year-round activity types under **What sounds fun?** and a suitable distance. Wait for the search to settle, then tap **Give us options**.
- Capture the full-screen **Date Night shortlist / Tonight's options** with genuine non-seasonal ideas.
- Important: in October, the home page's Halloween promotion panel remains even with Spooky Season off. This is expected. The real options overlay gives an evergreen view; do not remove the panel in an image editor, change the date, or use unpublished UI.
- Filename: `03-date-night-options-raw.png`.
- Draft alt text: “Date Night shortlist of year-round activities near the selected location.”

### 4. Nightlife: choose the kind of evening

- Close the overlay, then tap **Nightlife**.
- Set **How far?**, **Budget**, **What kind of place?** and **What's the vibe?** using the actual controls. The vibe slider runs from **Chill** to **Lively**; it is a preference, not a guarantee about a venue's current atmosphere.
- Capture the most readable normal view of the Nightlife identity and its controls. A real options view is an acceptable fallback if controls cannot form a useful viewport; record that change in the capture sheet.
- Filename: `04-nightlife-preferences-raw.png`.
- Draft alt text: “Nightlife preferences for venue type, travel distance, budget and a chill-to-lively evening.”

### 5. One Date Night destination, with useful details

- Return to **Date Night**, keep **Spooky Season** off, open **Give us options**, and tap a genuine year-round card. Alternatively, use **Pick our date** and wait until the choosing animation finishes.
- Capture **Tonight's date**, the real destination name and details. Scroll normally to include the directions area if it fits clearly.
- **Directions · Google Maps ↗** is an external link. **Website & info ↗** or **Check showtimes ↗** appears only for an eligible Date Night result with a URL. Do not add a missing website button or open an external service just for the screenshot.
- Retain any “Hours unconfirmed”/“verify before going” notice and third-party explanation visible in the chosen viewport. Do not remove or rewrite them.
- Filename: `05-date-night-result-raw.png`.
- Draft alt text: “A Date Night destination with its available details and external directions.” Mention a website only if actually shown.

### 6. Favorites: places worth keeping

- From a result you genuinely want in this demonstration, tap **Save**; the button changes to **Saved**. Repeat through the real UI for another useful place if desired.
- Close the result using **Close result**. If Dinner or Nightlife options remain underneath, also use **Close options**. Then tap **Favorites** in the bottom navigation. Capture the real saved list. Do not tap **Saved** again; that removes the favorite.
- Do not clear existing personal favorites or add personal comments to stage the shot. If existing entries are private, pause and choose a safe capture approach with the owner. A blank state can be documented but is a weaker listing image.
- Use Favorites rather than manufacturing history. History records explicit **We chose this / We did this / We went here → Save choice** actions; it is not an automatic archive of every result.
- Filename: `06-favorites-raw.png`.
- Draft alt text: “Favorites saved locally in Pick For Us for later decisions.”

- No personal messages, caller names, email addresses, account information, GPS coordinates or home address are visible.
- Keyboard, permission prompts, loading animations and notification overlays are gone. Do not photograph the handset; use native screenshots.
- Real limitations have not been hidden or retouched. If discovery fails, preserve that as QA evidence and retry normally later; do not turn the failure into a fabricated successful listing image.

## Export target: preserve the actual app

The plan is six opaque **1080×1920** portrait exports. A 20:9 raw phone image such as 1080×2400 exceeds Google's 2:1 maximum when uploaded unchanged. Keep the raw image; do not stretch it or crop away app controls to force 9:16.

Preferred final treatment is an intact, proportionally scaled capture on a restrained complementary canvas, with no device frame or invented controls. For example, an intact 1080×2400 capture scales to 864×1920, centered with 108 pixels on each side. This is a proposed layout, not a promise of recommendation eligibility: review readability at actual display size and retain enough visible app content. If text becomes too small, use a more suitable genuine capture viewport/device or choose a clearer actual screen. Do not upscale a low-resolution capture and call it higher-fidelity evidence.

A final export may remove irrelevant system-bar clutter as a documented presentation edit, while preserving the unedited original. Never modify app state, venue facts, warnings or controls. Prefer a clean capture to retouching. Do not fake battery/connectivity evidence; charge the phone and use its normal working connection. Any remaining privacy issue should trigger a recapture rather than invisible falsification.

## Requirements versus our chosen quality target

Google's phone screenshot requirements are JPEG or opaque 24-bit PNG, dimensions 320–3840 pixels, long side no more than twice the short side, and at least two screenshots overall; up to eight per device type. Screenshot-based recommendation formats for apps call for at least four 1080p images in 9:16 portrait or 16:9 landscape. Six is our chosen set, not Google's minimum. The guidance favors real app captures, UI-first opening images, restrained captions and short alt text. [S1]

Phone assets do not establish tablet support. No tablet screenshot, tablet test or other form-factor acceptance has been performed for this guide. Those remain deferred.

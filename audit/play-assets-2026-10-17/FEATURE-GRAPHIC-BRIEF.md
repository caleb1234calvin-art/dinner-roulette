## One practical feature-graphic concept

- Canvas: **1024×500**, opaque RGB PNG; an equivalent high-quality JPEG is allowed.
- Theme: an evergreen choice between food, a shared activity and a night out, using compatible established owner artwork where the rights review supports reuse.
- Composition: a simple central grouping of three visual cues, with subtle pointing/choice movement echoing the icon. Use coral and teal as accents, with a complementary background that stays distinct from the store surround.
- Keep the important group away from the edges. A central 800×340 working area is our conservative design aid, not an official Google safe-area specification. Inspect the actual surface crops before final selection.
- Avoid a giant duplicate app-icon tile. If a small product wordmark is useful, use the established spelling and style once. The illustration should carry the experience; no extra slogan is required.
- No invented app screen, device mockup, third-party venue logo, map-provider endorsement, review stars, rank/discount claim or install button. Do not make Halloween the principal design.
- Working filename: `pick-for-us-feature-en-US-1024x500-v1.png`.
- Draft alt text: “Food, a shared activity and a night out in the coral and teal style of Pick For Us.” Update to match the finished artwork.

This is a design brief, not generated art. Any later composition must be visually inspected and approved as a candidate; owner approval of the final visual is still needed before publication.

## Technical export checks

For the feature graphic, Google requires 1024×500 JPEG or opaque 24-bit PNG. Its guidance favors a central focus, complementary brand colors and no prominent duplication of the app icon. [S1]

The Play app icon is a separate export: **512×512**, **32-bit PNG**, **sRGB**, maximum **1024 KB**, full square without baked-in outer rounded corners or an outer drop shadow. Existing shading inside the artwork can stay. The Play store supplies its own mask/shadow. Do not substitute launcher/adaptive-icon layers for this listing asset. [S2]

Save originals, editable composition where available, final exports and hashes separately. Verify dimensions, color mode, alpha/profile, file size and visual quality; inspect both at full size and thumbnail size. Keep a record of any resize, crop or color conversion. No broad source or launcher-icon change is necessary merely to create a listing export.

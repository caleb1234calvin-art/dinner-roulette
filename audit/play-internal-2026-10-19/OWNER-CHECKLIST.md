## Internal versus closed testing

An app active **exclusively on internal testing** does not need the Data safety form. Moving to closed/open/production requires it. This is a form exemption, not blanket privacy or policy clearance. [Data safety scope](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)

The 12-testers/14-days gate applies to **personal accounts created after November 13, 2023**, before applying for production access. It requires at least 12 closed-test participants continuously opted in for the preceding 14 days, then a production-access application. Internal testing time does not substitute. Confirm account type/date before treating this as this owner's requirement. When moving a tester to closed testing, they must leave internal testing first. [Personal-account requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en), [Track eligibility](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en)

## Data safety working proposal: evidence, not submitted answers

Google's form includes controlled WebView traffic and SDKs. Local-only processing is excluded. Ephemeral off-device processing still belongs in form answers. Sharing exemptions need justification; provider branding alone does not establish a processor relationship. [Definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)

- **Location:** Current policy/source says unrounded returned coordinates go to first-party search, Nominatim and Overpass. Treat approximate and precise location as candidate collected types, for app functionality. Do not infer approximate-only from `enableHighAccuracy:false`. Final-device permission/traffic evidence must settle exact scope, optionality and any sharing exemption.
- **Analytics:** Current policy and prior reviewed operational evidence establish Vercel aggregate page views, geographic and device/browser information. Candidate types include app interactions and approximate location; assess request-derived identifiers against actual payloads/provider guidance. The mode-selection event exists in source, but successful intake remains unconfirmed. Do not answer “no data collected.”
- **Diagnostics/external resources:** Vercel hosting/diagnostics, Google Fonts and Grok resources receive request information. Determine actual diagnostics/identifier categories, recipients and retention from configuration/traffic. Script presence is not proof of advertising, sale or every possible cookie being accepted.
- **Local preferences/history/notes:** Current policy says these remain in browser/WebView storage without app-account synchronization. Exclude from off-device categories only while confirmed local-only; Android backup/device-transfer behavior still needs checking.
- **Support:** The mailto opens the user's email app without sending. If the user sends, Gmail/operator receive email and message content. Review applicability of email/support data categories for the final flow rather than falsely claiming the app automatically uploads messages.
- **Security/deletion:** Configured HTTPS and cleartext-disabled source support a security proposal, but do not certify all final traffic. Privacy contact is `causticrelay@gmail.com`; local clearing and operator requests have distinct scope. Do not promise universal 24-hour deletion or assert all providers erase immediately.

Before closed testing, finish actual per-type collected/shared/purpose/optional/ephemeral answers and account-creation applicability. The current account-free discovery UI does not prove historical account data never existed. Google requires in-app and external deletion initiation when in-app account creation is offered. Privacy policy must be publicly accessible, non-geofenced, non-PDF, and linked in-app and Console; it must identify the app/developer, handling, contact, retention and deletion. [User Data full policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en)

## Washington reconciliation

Washington State Gambling Commission states that 23 tribes operate 29 casinos under tribal-state compacts. Its current live casino-location directory renders 28 entries; Shoalwater Bay Casino is the operating property omitted from that rendered result set and is separately confirmed by the current casino/operator presence. Pass 21 therefore activates 29 Washington tribal casino destinations. Commercial house-banked card rooms remain outside the project's casino-destination scope.

## Oregon reconciliation

Oregon compact and tribal-gaming materials support eight tribes currently operating casino gaming. Burns Paiute's Old Camp Casino remains closed. Current operator/NIGC material also reflects two multi-property operators: the Confederated Tribes of Coos, Lower Umpqua and Siuslaw Indians operate Three Rivers destinations in Florence and Coos Bay, while the Coquille Indian Tribe now operates Ko-Kwel Casino Resort destinations in Coos Bay and Medford. The Medford property is a current Class II casino destination and is included under the project's tribal casino-style venue scope. Pass 21 therefore activates ten Oregon casino destinations.

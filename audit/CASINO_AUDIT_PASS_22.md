## South Dakota reconciliation

South Dakota is represented by eighteen current Deadwood casino destinations plus eight tribal casino destinations physically located in the state. The Deadwood tourism directory contains some duplicate/embedded gaming listings, so pass 22 reconciles destination-level properties rather than treating every directory entry as a separate casino. Bodega Casino is folded into the Buffalo-Bodega Gaming Complex; A & B Pizza at Oyster Bay and Deadwood Badlands/Brothel Bar are not promoted as standalone casino destinations. Rosebud Casino is intentionally not counted in South Dakota: its physical casino property is south of the border near Valentine, Nebraska, and it is already represented in the Nebraska pass.

## North Dakota reconciliation

The North Dakota Attorney General's gaming oversight material states that the state conducts inspections at five Indian casinos. Pass 22 activates those five physical casino destinations: Dakota Magic Casino & Hotel, Spirit Lake Casino & Resort, 4 Bears Casino & Lodge, Sky Dancer Casino & Resort, and Prairie Knights Casino & Resort. Charitable gaming, OTB sites, and non-destination gaming outlets remain outside the curated casino backbone.

## Scope notes

- Physical casino destinations only; online gaming, ordinary charitable gaming sites, and embedded convenience-style gaming are excluded.
- Cross-border properties are assigned to their physical state to avoid duplicate jurisdiction counts.
- Property coordinates are included for discovery/routing but no coordinate-source field is asserted where property-specific provenance was not strong enough to record.
- `audit/casino-sources-pass-22.json` is a supplemental manifest read alongside the historical and pass-21 manifests.

# Independent review and acceptance qualifications

A separate reviewer challenged all four source reports and requirement matrices. Their material findings were accepted with the qualifications below. The separate [final acceptance receipt](INDEPENDENT-ACCEPTANCE.md) records the final consistency review. This does not certify legal compliance or grant release approval.

1. Privacy FAIL is the incomplete in-app policy material, not absence of a link alone. A policy link or text can satisfy that presentation requirement. Unknown Console URL remains HOLD.
2. Internal LEGAL.md analytics wording is stale; it does not by itself prove a false public promise. Analytics source integration does not establish successful transmission or retention.
3. Full returned coordinates are forwarded; approximate-only processing cannot be inferred from enableHighAccuracy:false or the reverse-geocoder label zoom.
4. Account deletion is conditional on actual in-app account creation. A dependency or server module alone does not establish that trigger.
5. Casino discovery has no identified wagering flow in the inspected source. License, adult-only and gambling-ad conditions are not universal casino-mention rules.
6. WebViews are not categorically prohibited. Ownership, meaningful utility, hosted changes and actual Android behavior still matter.
7. API 36 is the current ordinary-mobile new-app/update threshold; source meets it. Final artifact verification remains separate.
8. Historical AAB/native-input parity is useful bounded evidence. It is not a fresh main build, signed release, PR #75 web deployment, physical-device test or Play approval.
9. Play stores/manages/uses its signing key; “owns” overstates the cited terms. Actual enrollment, certificate count and hybrid state are unverified. Separate upload and distribution keys are recommended; no categorical rejection of every same-key arrangement is claimed.
10. Current package identity is valid. A brand-only preference is an owner decision, not a compliance repair; private registration/version history cannot be proved from public search.
11. Broad FileProvider paths and backup-transfer ambiguity are P2 hardening issues, without a demonstrated exploit. The inspected AAB contains no native .so files, but runtime compatibility remains untested.
12. Export classification, exemptions and filing duties cannot be inferred from encryption/library presence alone. Current regulation is narrower than simplified guidance in material respects; no app-specific legal conclusion is assigned.
13. Rights/rating/ads holds require evidence and truthful declarations, not allegations of infringement or ads. Original creator provenance and applicable generation/license terms can support rights. Factual brand references do not automatically require permission.
14. Saved search location has no source expiry. The narrowly labeled favorites/history reset omits it without promising full erasure. Address retention/control facts in policy and testing.
15. Exact device matrices and production architecture sign-off are audit assurance controls rather than individually prescribed Google tests. The full Academy course was unavailable, with no separate course-completion release prerequisite established.

The consolidated P0/P1 priorities identify submission gates and preserve original lane priorities in JSON. P0 does not mean a security emergency. The overall recommendation is HOLD until applicable evidence and corrections pass independent review and owner-controlled release decisions occur.

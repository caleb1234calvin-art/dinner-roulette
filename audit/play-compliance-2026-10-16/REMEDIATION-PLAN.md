# Prioritized remediation plan

## P0 Critical submission blocker

**Complete the user-facing privacy policy.** Status FAIL for the inspected in-app text. Establish the operator/contact, categories and purposes, recipients/providers, security practices, retention and deletion facts. Publish accessible, accurate policy text or a link in the app; separately verify the public Console policy URL. Do not invent retention promises or processor classifications. Acceptance requires source and real-device/live-page inspection plus reconciliation to runtime evidence. Original privacy and independent lane priority: P1; consolidated priority P0 reflects submission criticality only.

## P1 Required corrections and evidence gates

- **Data Safety and permissions:** trace the selected Android/hosted release's actual network flows, coordinate granularity, analytics, scripts, logs and account behavior. Resolve provider roles and retention. Reconcile each proposed answer with evidence and applicable exceptions; owner reviews the real form.
- **Identity and artifact:** decide the exact package before first registration, check historical uploads/version codes, bind final AAB/hash/certificate to hosted deployment, and verify cryptographic signing and intended public certificate when separately authorized.
- **Android acceptance:** test install/update, foreground location alternatives and denial, offline/retry, Back/lifecycle, external links, keyboard/insets and core modes on the final combination. Browser tests do not substitute for native acceptance.
- **Store content and rights:** review final listing/screenshots, rating questionnaire, audience/countries and any ads/monetization; produce provenance/permission records for assets. Casino discovery alone does not justify a fabricated license or 18+ requirement.
- **Account and legal facts:** owner verifies developer identity, account authority, accepted agreements/notices, actual Play App Signing enrollment and certificate inventory. Owner/adviser resolves actual product export/sanctions scope; no ECCN, exemption or filing duty is assigned here.

Unknown Console facts are HOLD, not FAIL. P1 evidence gates block a submission decision even where no code defect is established. They are not all prerequisites to ordinary development-only builds.

## P2 Hardening and documentation

- Decide whether the remotely hosted Capacitor architecture is supported and reliable enough for release; document bridge/navigation/review-access risks and runtime change control.
- Narrow or remove unused FileProvider paths after checking file chooser needs; test URI-grant restrictions.
- Define Android 12+ cloud/device-transfer rules and align any local-only privacy claims with actual backup behavior.
- Verify expected upload-certificate identity, not only the existence of a signature; inspect actual workflow protection and custody without exposing secrets.
- Update internal analytics documentation and replace “Google Play owns” the distribution key with accurate management/storage language. Neither wording proves a public misrepresentation or contract breach.
- Review applicable agreement notices before owner action. Do not automatically rotate keys or change package identity.

These may be isolated follow-ups. No policy violation or exploit is asserted merely from a hardening recommendation.

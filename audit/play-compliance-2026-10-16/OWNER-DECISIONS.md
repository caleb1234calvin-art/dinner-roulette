# Owner decisions needed

No agreement, declaration, credential action or release approval is made by this checklist.

1. **Operator and distribution:** What legal entity/account is publishing, who can bind it, which countries will be targeted, and are there existing package registrations/uploads or accepted terms/notices?
2. **Audience and content:** What is the intended age audience and monetization model? Supply the final listing, screenshots, content-rating answers and asset rights/provenance. Do not infer an age floor solely from casino venue discovery.
3. **Privacy facts:** Confirm a public privacy contact, each provider/processor and purpose, actual analytics/logging, retention/deletion practices, and whether user account creation is reachable in the intended Android product.
4. **Package identity:** Prefer app.pickforus.android for a new brand-only identity only after domain/brand control, availability and first-registration status are checked. Otherwise retain com.calebcalvin.pickforus unless a deliberate new-app migration is intended. Neither availability nor ownership is established through public absence.
5. **Signing strategy:** Decide Play-only versus independent cross-store signing needs, who retains the upload key, backup/recovery responsibilities and the actual Play signing route. Inspect public certificate fingerprints and enrollment state; do not send private keys or passwords with the audit.
6. **Export/legal assessment:** Establish operator jurisdiction, actual distributed Android/browser code, hosted-service functionality, destinations and relevant component classification evidence. Obtain qualified export/legal advice for unresolved issues. HTTPS/open-source libraries do not by themselves establish classification or exemption.
7. **Correction candidate:** Provide the missing facts so the already-authorized isolated privacy/transparency work can proceed. An exact package-identifier decision is still needed before changing identity. Keep audit main, production and PR #75 unchanged while isolated work is reviewed.

Owner review of these facts does not authorize production signing, uploading, legal acceptance, merging or deployment. Those actions retain their own approval boundaries.

Select Fridays & Saturdays September 19 – October 31, plus Sunday, October 25

The thrills are about to get terrifying. And yes, all of the haunted mazes are included in admission!

Whether you're looking for family-friendly Halloween fun during the day or heart-pounding scares after sunset, Halloween at Worlds of Fun delivers screams around every corner.

Fifteen Haunted Experiences

The fear is here.

Experience immersive haunted mazes filled with unexpected scares, terrifying creatures and chilling surprises, plus chilling shows and scare zones with no escape.

Six Horrifying Haunted Mazes, Included with Admission

BloodShed

Visit the Old MacDonald Meat Processing plant where they’re slashing more than just their prices for the perfect cut.

Ripper Alley

Step lively into the shadowy streets of Victorian London for a journey into murder, madness… and things far more terrifying than the night itself.

Pumpkin Eater

Venture into a twisted farm where rotting pumpkins, scarecrows, and an abandoned township hide unspeakable horrors around every corner. Then face a terrifying pumpkin as the harvest comes alive.

Lore of the Vampire

Visit a maze of vampires under the shadow of night in their search for the liquid of the living.

Blood on the Bayou

Navigate the bog and the manor house where curses possess and demons trudge, seeking their next soul.

Zombie High

The bell has rung and the students and staff of Cleaver High are ready to administer a test for your life!

Haunted Mazes are included with admission to the park. Skip the regular lines by adding Fright Lane to your visit!

Four Scare Zones

The monsters are loose.

Navigate through themed scare zones, including two all-new zones, where ghouls, monsters and creatures lurk around every turn.

NEW! Mr. Tickle’s Sideshow Alley

Expelled from the big top, meet Mr. Tickle’s band of merry malcontents as they welcome you to their delirious sideshow

NEW! Invasion

A horrible infestation has taken over the Serengeti Research Facility. Everything that crawls lurks in the shadows.

NEW! Necropolis

Explore an ancient cemetery where there’s always room for more residents.

Outlaw’s Revenge

Find gunslingers and bandits as you wander the dusty streets of this parched land.

 

Live Entertainment

The screams don't stop with the scares.

Enjoy exclusive Halloween Haunt performances featuring:

NEW! Spellbound

As darkness is cast over Haunt, everyone's favorite Haunt Hellions are hosting and bringing new trouble to the night! With a collection of spellbinding tunes and appearances by some hair-raising characters this is one spectacle you won’t want to miss! (Located in Gateway Gardens)

Mr. Tickle’s Last Laff

Welcome back to the big top! With his painted-on grin, Mr. Tickle runs his circus with a simple vow... no tyranny, only chaos...New for 2026, Mr. Tickle, Madame Lionne, and their clown posse are ready to unleash something altogether different and playfully twisted during Halloween Haunt! (Located in Country Junction)

Overlord’s Awakening

Behold the spectacle of the Overlord’s Awakening as he brings forth the terror into Worlds of Fun’s Halloween Haunt. No one is safe as he calls forth his minions and transforms the night at Haunt. (Located in Gateway Gardens)

Conjure the Night

Your deepest, darkest fears take center stage with Conjure the Night, Haunt’s live Halloween spectacle. Immerse yourself in the electric pulse, rockin’ vocals and soul shattering rhythms of Halloween’s most terrifying elements in a battle of Good vs. Evil. (Located in Gateway Gardens)

Haunted Homecoming

These teenage deadheads take you on a rotten rock and roll revue through the hallowed halls of Cleaver High. From the rousing vocals of yesteryear to the wild and wacky spectral tunes of today, the undead and recently reanimated students invite you to their yearly return during this Haunted Homecoming! (Located in Moulin Rouge theater)

Seasonal Food & Drinks

Sink your teeth into limited-time, hauntingly delicious flavors.

Featured offerings include:

  • Murder Mystery Shake: Strawberry shake topped with gummy body parts (at Sand Dune Diner)
  • Detroit Style BBQ Pizza: Thick crust with mozzarella & cheddar, smoky burnt ends, creamy mac sauce, BBQ sauce and red onion (at River City Grill)
  • Funeral Potatoes: Potato slices smothered in cheese sauce and topped with sauteed onions and dried chives (at River City Grill)
  • Swamp Monster Burger: Juicy burger topped with creamy spinach artichoke dip (at G'Rilla Grill)
  • Monster Munch Popcorn: Orange, purple and green popcorn with a touch of sweetness (at Coke Marketplace, G'Rilla Grill, Sand Dune Diner and River City Grill)
  • Dirty Sodas: A selection of dirty sodas—choose from Berry Bliss, Coconut Twist, Butterscotch Kiss or Orange Fizz (at Swedish Dippin' Chalet)
  • Specialty beers and cocktails for Guests age 21 and up

Halloween Haunt Tips

  • Arrive early to maximize your visit.
  • Haunted attractions are most popular after sunset.
  • Check the daily show schedule upon arrival.
  • Purchase Fast Lane and Fright Lane in advance when available.

Know Before You Go

  • There is no re-entry after 6:00pm.
  • Costumes, masks and full-face makeup are not permitted during Haunt.
  • Scare zones contain fog, special effects, loud noises, and intense themes.
  • Some attractions may not be appropriate for young children.
  • Event offerings are subject to change without notice.
  • Please review our chaperone policy here.

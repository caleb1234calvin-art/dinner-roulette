Amenities*with purchase of wash

  • Molded mat washer

    Keep your contoured floor mats clean with our free molded mat washers*, designed to deep clean grooves and raised edges. WeatherTech approved.

  • Detail Kits

    Tommy's Express Detail Kits include everything you need to clean and refresh your car's interior, with glass and general purpose cleaner and a microfiber towel.

  • Flat mat washer

    Keep your flat floor mats clean with our free flat mat washers*, made for a quick, even rinse that leaves mats fresh and ready to go. 

  • Vacuums

    Our free vacuum* systems were designed to be effective and efficient, the perfect pair with your wash.

  • Compressed air

    Free compressed air stations* quickly remove water, dust, and debris from hard-to-reach areas for a clean, finished look.

  • Tommy Essential Vending

    Stock up on all your essentials with convenient vending machines right at the car wash. 

  • Express car wash

    Experience a safe, express clean in just 3 minutes using gentle soft cloth for a spotless shine every time.

  • Express Interior

    Our team vacuums, wipes down surfaces, cleans glass, and refreshes the inside of your vehicle in just 10 minutes.

  • EV Charging

    Our highspeed EV charging stations let you conveniently charge your electric vehicle while you visit the wash.

  • Disposable towels

    Dry off door jambs, clean up spills, and finish your wheels with our disposable towels right in the vacuum bay.

  • Microfiber towels

    Dust your interior and shine your windows with our microfiber towels.

About Tommy's Express O'Fallon

Welcome to Tommy's Express at 101 Fallon Loop Rd. O'Fallon, MO 63368 in O'Fallon! We're proud to serve you and your vehicle with the best car wash in O'Fallon. At Tommy's Express, we’re more than just a wash — we believe in enriching lives, giving back to our communities, and delivering an exceptional clean.

What you can expect at Tommy's Express in O'Fallon:

  • Fast 3-minute express tunnel wash. Quick, convenient, and consistent.
  • Unlimited and PayPerWash options. Customize your membership experience.
  • Vacuums and mat washers. Keep your interior spotless.
  • TommyClub app lane. Skip the line and pay from your phone.
  • Detail Kits available for purchase. Take your clean to the next level.
  • Plans for every driver. Perfect for individuals, families, or fleets.
  • Fleet and business plans available. Save more with discounted pricing for high-volume washing.

We're locally operated and proud to be part of the O'Fallon community. Through our Serving Communities initiative, we focus on making a meaningful impact:

  • Supporting clean water initiatives through partners like Water Mission.
  • Partnering with local organizations for fundraisers, events, and community support.
  • Reducing environmental impact through water reclamation and eco-friendly practices.

With over 50 years of innovation, Tommy's Express continues to deliver the best car wash experience that fits your busy lifestyle. Our 101 Fallon Loop Rd. O'Fallon, MO 63368 location is easy to find near . We hope to see you soon!

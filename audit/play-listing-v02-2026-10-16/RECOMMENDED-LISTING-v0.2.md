## Full description (1,804 characters)

Not sure where to go? Pick For Us can help.

Whether you're choosing somewhere to eat, planning a date, or looking for something to do tonight, Pick For Us helps you explore your options and make a decision.

Choose what you're interested in, set your preferences, and let the app suggest somewhere to go.

Find something that fits your plans

Dinner — Explore nearby restaurants and filter by cuisine, price, distance, and other preferences.

Date Night — Discover activities and destinations for time together, including seasonal attractions when available.

Nightlife — Explore nightlife options by venue type, distance and budget, with a chill-to-lively preference.

Make the choice your own

- Get one suggested destination or a shortlist of up to four options.
- Enter a location manually or use your device's location with permission.
- Save favorites and keep a history of choices you log.
- Set aside places you're not interested in visiting tonight.
- Adjust your preferences whenever you like.
- Open directions using external services, and venue websites where available in Date Night.
- Choose between light and dark themes.

A little less deciding. A little more doing.

Pick For Us doesn't promise the perfect destination. It gives you a way to explore available choices and find something that interests you.

Favorites, logged choices, and some preferences are saved locally on your device rather than synced through an account. Internet access is needed to load the live experience and refresh results. Available places and activities depend on location and data coverage, and venue information can change.

Always confirm important details, such as operating hours, prices, and admission requirements, with the venue before visiting.

Pick For Us — made to help you decide what's next.

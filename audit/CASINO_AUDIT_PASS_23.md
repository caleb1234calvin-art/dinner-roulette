## Florida reconciliation

Florida Gaming Control Commission materials identify eight licensed pari-mutuel facilities in Miami-Dade and Broward counties where slot-machine gaming is authorized. Pass 23 activates those eight physical casino destinations and does not promote racing/cardroom-only pari-mutuel facilities.

Current NIGC and tribal/operator materials separately reconcile eight tribal casino destinations: six Seminole Gaming properties and two Miccosukee properties. The Seminole roster includes the current Brighton Bay replacement property. Little Trail Casino is retained as a distinct destination because Miccosukee describes it as a separately built and branded 8,500-square-foot casino next to the service plaza rather than merely gaming machines embedded in the plaza.

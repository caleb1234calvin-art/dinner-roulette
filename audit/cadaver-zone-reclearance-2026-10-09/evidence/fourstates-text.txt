Spookthefourstates.com - Spook Houses, Haunted Houses & Other Spooky Stuff in the Joplin, NWA, Four State Area Find what SCARES you! This site was born in 2010 out of our frustrations in trying to find ALL haunts and spooktacular attractions in the Joplin, Missouri Four State Area. This site contains all-inclusive haunt information in one centralized and user friendly location. It's a free listing site of haunts that want to utilize us! We took it a step further and added a personal rating scale constructed and tailored to our love of all things that ooze, slash, grunt, growl, scream, howl, and go bump in the night! We try to keep the info on here as correct and current as possible. Some of these places re-open evey season, some come and go. So, if you see something that's wrong or if something on the site is not working, please let us know. The year after the name is the last time the info was update, please check their website or facebook pages before you head out spooking. Feel free to reach out to us with updates to haunts and ideas on making this site a 'FANGtastic' destination for all your haunting thrills and chills!

The tickets do NOT need to be bought in advance, only the group booked. Click the tab below to reserve your group! Book Group Now Prepare For The Maze WHAT TO WEAR? We recommend that you wear clothing appropriate to the weather and remember that we are an outdoors activity and our trail could be muddy if it has rained recently. Wear casual comfortable clothing Bring a jacket if you plan to stay into the evening Wear comfortable walking shoes or boots ​ You may want to apply these safeguards before entering the Maze: Bug spray Sunscreen Items you may want to bring with you: Your camera Flashlight Walkie Talkies or Cell Phones to talk with your family and friends *Flashlights are available for purchase at the Maze. CORN MAZE RULES ​ Corn Maze Rules: (not following the rules could result in removal from or denied entry into the maze) ​ Only walk on the trail provided No picking ears of corn or tearing down corn plants. No throwing corn ears or kernels. No SMOKING! No ALCOHOL! No PETS! No concealed weapons No running and no climbing on bridges and fences. No outside food or drink allowed. All purses, backpacks, or bags are subject to being searched. Ready to Join the fun?

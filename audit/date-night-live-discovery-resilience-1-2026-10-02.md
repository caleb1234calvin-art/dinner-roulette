## Implemented design

The same four configured mirrors start at approximately 0/1,500/3,000/4,500ms for each needed group. A hard failure advances immediately. First valid empty or nonempty response wins; losing requests abort, late results cannot mutate accepted data, and all timers/listeners/controllers clean up. Own promise timers bound header/body stalls even when mocked transport ignores abort. At most four groups and four mirrors per group are allowed: 16 attempts, no infinite retries or added providers.

**Deadline policy remains unchanged:** one 20,000ms absolute server/provider budget, maximum 8,000ms per attempt and 25,000ms client watchdog. No extension has been justified or applied. With all four groups started promptly, all-stall requests normally exhaust their full attempts at 12,500ms; the absolute 20s ceiling remains for delayed starts. The client still explicitly clears loading at its watchdog, invalidates before aborting, forwards the RPC signal, ignores stale/late results and permits retry independently of transport cooperation.

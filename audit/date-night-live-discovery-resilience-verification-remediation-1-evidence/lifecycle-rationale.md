## Explicit boundary and preserved semantics

The existing Halloween OFF classifier drops records whose only reconstructed category is seasonal. Therefore an ordinary active representation and a purely seasonal negative representation do not merge OFF, for either Anything or an ordinary subset. The new boundary regression proves these two selections remain equivalent and neither acquires affirmative seasonal data nor returns eligible seasonal output. This change does not claim universal protection across inactive seasonal classification. Ordinary-to-ordinary cross-category lifecycle protection is separately proved OFF and ON.

Unrelated negative records do not become eligible positives. Regressions include distant negative records and a Pumpkin negative at the same coordinates as an unrelated active Corn identity; the latter is not merged because no existing name/type identity rule matches. Existing identity heuristics and thresholds remain unchanged.

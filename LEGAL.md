# Dinner Roulette — Legal & Third-Party Notice

_Last updated: September 9, 2026_

Dinner Roulette is an independent discovery and decision-making application. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or operated by the restaurants, venues, delivery services, rideshare services, mapping providers, casinos, or other third parties referenced in the app unless an affiliation is expressly stated.

## Third-party services and outbound-link policy

Dinner Roulette may provide optional links that open third-party websites or applications, including mapping, food-delivery, rideshare, restaurant, entertainment, ticketing, and venue services. These links are provided for convenience only. A link does not imply a partnership, sponsorship, endorsement, or guarantee that the destination supports a particular restaurant, venue, ride, product, or transaction.

Third-party integrations should default to neutral Dinner Roulette presentation, plain-text identification, and ordinary outbound links or documented/developer-approved linking mechanisms. Do not imitate third-party trade dress or present a third-party service as an integrated Dinner Roulette product without authorization.

Third-party services operate under their own terms, privacy policies, eligibility requirements, pricing, fees, availability, and safety rules. Dinner Roulette does not control those services and does not process purchases, delivery orders, rides, reservations, tickets, wagers, or other transactions completed through them.

Do not scrape, reproduce, mirror, frame, or republish third-party menus, prices, service content, account information, or proprietary datasets merely because Dinner Roulette links to the service. Provider APIs or other licensed data sources require a separate terms review before integration.

## Trademarks, imagery, and branding

Names and trademarks belonging to third parties remain the property of their respective owners. References to third-party brands should be limited to identifying the relevant business, destination, or service and should not suggest affiliation.

Official logos, badges, branded buttons, trade dress, promotional artwork, photographs, or other copyrighted/brand-controlled assets should not be added solely because they are publicly visible online. Before adding a third-party brand asset, confirm that Dinner Roulette has a license, permission, an applicable provider brand guideline, or another defensible right to use it.

Dinner Roulette-owned, commissioned, licensed, or appropriately sourced generic category artwork is preferred where an official brand asset is not necessary.

## Restaurant and venue information

Restaurant, venue, price, rating, distance, hours, availability, address, and similar information can change. Dinner Roulette is a discovery tool, not a guarantee that displayed information is complete, current, or error-free. Users should verify time-sensitive details with the applicable restaurant, venue, or service before relying on them.

Do not state or imply that a restaurant is currently available on a delivery platform, that a venue is open, that a ticket is available, or that a ride can be obtained unless the claim is supported by an authorized current data source. Prefer language such as “Search,” “Check,” or “Open” for external-service shortcuts.

## Data provenance

Curated catalogs should retain enough source/provenance information to allow important factual claims to be rechecked. Public business facts may change, and source records should distinguish verified/curated information from fallback, estimated, user-entered, or discovery-derived information where practical.

Do not ingest personal data, proprietary databases, copyrighted compilations, or restricted third-party content into Dinner Roulette merely because it can be accessed on the public web. New automated data sources should receive a source and terms review before becoming a production dependency.

## Location, privacy, and device data

Dinner Roulette may use location information when the user permits it in order to find and rank nearby places. Location permission should be requested only when needed for a location-dependent feature and should not be described as more precise or persistent than the implementation actually provides.

App preferences, favorites, exclusions, and history may be stored locally on the user's device. If future versions add accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, or server-side storage of user or location data, this notice and the in-app privacy disclosure must be reviewed before release.

Third-party destinations opened from Dinner Roulette may receive information from the user's browser, operating system, or the third-party application and may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

## Security and external navigation

External web links opened in a new browsing context should use appropriate isolation attributes such as `noopener` and `noreferrer` where compatible. User-controlled or remotely supplied URLs should be validated before they are rendered as navigable links.

## No professional advice

Dinner Roulette provides general discovery information and entertainment-oriented recommendations. It does not provide legal, financial, medical, transportation, gambling, or safety advice.

## Release checklist for new integrations

Before adding a new external platform or data provider:

1. Identify the service and the exact type of link/API/data access being used.
2. Check the provider's current terms and applicable developer/brand rules.
3. Use neutral Dinner Roulette styling unless branded assets are expressly permitted.
4. Avoid language implying partnership, endorsement, live availability, or guaranteed service.
5. Record data provenance where the integration contributes factual catalog information.
6. Update privacy disclosures if the integration changes what user or location data is collected, transmitted, or stored.
7. Recheck casino, alcohol, age-restricted, payment, and transportation features for additional regulatory requirements.

## Corrections and review

Listings, links, provider rules, and laws may change. Incorrect or outdated information should be corrected in the Dinner Roulette catalog when discovered. Material new integrations and any move toward commercialization, advertising, affiliate revenue, payments, reservations, wagering, or collection of sensitive/user-identifying data should receive a fresh compliance review and, where appropriate, review by qualified legal counsel.
